How does our website function?

It is a multi-functional platform designed to handle all aspects of resume management and processing. Here's how it works:

1. Resume Generation: Users can upload their resumes directly. For those who do not have a resume, the platform leverages AI to generate one based on user-provided information.

2. Question Generation: After analyzing the uploaded or generated resumes, the generates tailored quizzes and questions to assess the user's skills and knowledge.

3. Resume Screening System: The platform includes a robust screening mechanism to evaluate resumes, helping employers or recruiters identify suitable candidates efficiently. This comprehensive system simplifies the job application and recruitment process by combining advanced resume generation, skill assessment, and screening into a single platform.
