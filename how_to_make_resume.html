How to Write a Resume in 9 Easy Steps

Step 1: Choose an Appropriate Format

There are three standard formats:

  • Chronological: Focuses on work experience.
  • Functional: Highlights skills and abilities.
  • Combination: Balances work history and skills equally.

For most job seekers, the chronological resume is recommended.

Step 2: Style Your Resume

Use a well-designed template to make your resume stand out while keeping it professional.

Step 3: Add Contact Info to the Header

Include:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number
  • Professional email address

Avoid including personal details like full address, birth date, or zodiac sign.

Step 4: Write a Compelling Professional Summary

Your professional summary should be a 3-5 sentence introduction, highlighting your key skills and achievements.

If you are new to the workforce, consider using a resume objective instead.

Step 5: Describe Your Work History

List your work experience in reverse-chronological order. Include quantifiable achievements, such as:

  • Increased customer satisfaction by 20%
  • Reduced processing time by 30%

If you have no experience, emphasize education, volunteer work, or internships.

Step 6: Emphasize Your Skills

Highlight both hard skills (technical abilities) and soft skills (communication, teamwork, etc.).

Here’s a comparison:

  • Hard Skills: Programming, Machine Learning, Data Analysis, Technical Writing.
  • Soft Skills: Leadership, Adaptability, Problem-Solving, Communication.

Step 7: List Your Education and Credentials

Include:

  • Name and location of the school.
  • Degrees, honors, or relevant coursework.

Avoid including your GPA unless it's 3.5 or higher.

Step 8: Add Optional Sections

Optional sections help personalize your resume. Consider adding:

  • Volunteer work
  • Certifications & Licenses
  • Languages
  • Awards & Honors

Step 9: Proofread and Save as a PDF

Review your resume multiple times to avoid typos or formatting errors. Save it as a PDF for better formatting retention.

Key Takeaways

  • Choose the right resume format.
  • Tailor your resume for each job.
  • Use powerful action verbs.
  • Quantify your achievements.
  • Consider adding a cover letter.
  • Proofread multiple times before submitting.
